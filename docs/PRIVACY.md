# Privacy Policy & Data Handling

SMR Mirror is designed with a **Privacy-First** architecture.

- **No Cloud Recording**: Screen video frames travel directly between your phone and laptop over end-to-end encrypted WebRTC (`DTLS-SRTP`).
- **No Clipboard Telemetry**: Clipboard data is transferred strictly peer-to-peer over WebRTC DataChannel.
- **Session Control**: You can revoke connection authorization at any time with a single tap on your smartphone or laptop.
